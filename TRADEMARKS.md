# Trademark policy

> **Draft — not yet legal-reviewed.** This policy may change. It is not legal advice.

"NTX", "Network Text Exchange", "NTX compatible", and the NTX logo are marks of
CamelCase Technologies Ltd. We want everyone to be able to talk about and use the protocol. This
policy keeps the names meaningful.

## You may

- Use "NTX" and the logo to refer to the protocol, for example "supports the NTX protocol" or "an
  NTX library".
- Write about NTX in articles, talks, and documentation.
- Use the logo files in the [`assets`](https://github.com/ntxprotocol/ntx/tree/main/assets) folder of
  the `ntx` repository without changing them.

## "NTX compatible"

Use "NTX compatible" only for products that pass the NTX conformance suite, for the version of
the protocol you state. If your product stops passing, stop using the term.

## You may not

- Use the marks in a way that suggests we made, approved, or endorse your product or company.
- Use "NTX" in a company name, product name, or domain name in a way that could confuse people.
- Change the logo, or make a new logo that looks like it.

## Forks

You are free to fork our code under its licence. A fork that changes the protocol must be renamed
and must not use "NTX" as its name or claim to be compatible.

Email **<conduct@ntxprotocol.com>** until a dedicated address exists.
