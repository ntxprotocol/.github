# Governance

How decisions are made about the protocol is described in the specification repository:
**[spec/GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/ntxprotocol/spec/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md)**. It follows
the Community Specification Governance Policy 1.0.

Other repositories in this organisation follow their own maintainers' decisions, within the rules in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Protocol
behaviour is always decided in `spec`.

## Stewardship

NTX was started by CamelCase Technologies Ltd. The goal is a neutral standard, not a product
feature. Once there are other serious adopters, we intend to move NTX to a neutral foundation,
chosen together with those adopters.
