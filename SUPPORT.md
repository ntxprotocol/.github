# Support

## Questions and ideas

- **About the protocol:** open a *Discussion* issue in the
  [`spec`](https://github.com/ntxprotocol/spec/issues) repository.
- **About a library, app, or plugin:** open an issue in that repository.
- Please search existing issues first.

## Bugs

Open an issue in the repository where you found the bug. Say what you did, what you expected, and
what happened. Include your version and operating system.

## Security problems

Do not open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Conduct problems

Email **<conduct@ntxprotocol.com>**. See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Support is by volunteers

NTX is young and run by a small team. We reply when we can, and there is no guaranteed response time
for general questions.
